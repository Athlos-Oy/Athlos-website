# DC-Air WhatsApp saved replies

Paste-ready replies for the most common customer questions. Each one gives a
one-line answer plus a deep link that opens the exact answer on the Training
Center (the customer must be signed in once on that device; the link then
works directly). Save these in WhatsApp: Settings → Business tools → Quick
replies, or keep this file open.

Every reply you send with a link teaches the customer that the page is where
answers live — that is the point.

---

**1. No image appeared / "kuva ei tullut"**
> Don't retake! The image is saved in the sensor. In TWAIN press "Download Last Image". Full steps: https://athlos.fi/dc-air-training#faq-no-image

**2. Connection keeps dropping**
> Usually antenna or dock placement. Check these 6 things: https://athlos.fi/dc-air-training#faq-ble-drops

**3. Yellow light blinking / battery low**
> Dock the sensor for 15 min — that's enough for a full mouth series. Details: https://athlos.fi/dc-air-training#faq-battery-low

**4. Sensor not responding / seems dead**
> It's asleep. Put it on the dock for 10 seconds. More: https://athlos.fi/dc-air-training#faq-sleep-mode

**5. Image grainy or noisy**
> That's under-exposure — use 70 kV for adults and a slightly longer time. Steps: https://athlos.fi/dc-air-training#faq-noisy-image

**6. Lines/artifacts in the image**
> Calibration file issue — takes 2 minutes to fix: https://athlos.fi/dc-air-training#faq-line-artifacts

**7. Which disinfectants are OK?**
> Approved list (CaviWipes, Isopropyl 70% etc.) here: https://athlos.fi/dc-air-training#faq-disinfectants — never submerge or autoclave!

**8. Does it work with [imaging software]?**
> Almost certainly yes — DC-Air uses standard TWAIN. Demo videos for the common ones: https://athlos.fi/dc-air-training#integrations

**9. How do I position for [bitewing/anterior/etc.]?**
> Watch the 1–2 min positioning video: https://athlos.fi/dc-air-training#library

**10. New assistant / new clinic getting started**
> Start here — two short videos cover everything: https://athlos.fi/dc-air-training#start-here — and print the chairside card for the operatory wall.
